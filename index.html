Data provenance

All visualisations draw from three complementary sources, collected and cross-referenced in April 2026.

🛒
Steam Store API

Game metadata, review scores, and ownership estimates pulled directly from Valve's public endpoints. Covers titles with at least 10 user reviews.

~50,000 titles
📊
SteamSpy

Estimated player counts, CCU history, and genre tagging sourced from SteamSpy's community-maintained dataset. Used for retention curves.

13 months · Apr 2025 – Apr 2026
📁
Kaggle Dataset

Supplemental publisher / developer relationship data and historical pricing from a curated Kaggle export. Cross-validated against live API responses.
